Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email sivilpenguen@gmail.com — we usually answer within 2 days.

I bought something but don't have it

Open the game → Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I remove ads?

The Castle Key (full version) removes interstitial ads. Rewarded videos are always optional.

I don't want tips

Tap Turn Off Tips on any tip card, or switch them off in Settings → Tips.

Change language

Settings → Language (English, Türkçe, Español, Français).
