Castle Bop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Overview

Castle Bop ("the App") is developed by rakuappdigital. This policy explains what information is processed when you play the App and by which services. We do not run our own servers for the App and we do not collect personal data ourselves.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No account or sign-up
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or contacts
  • No location data
  • No analytics of our own and no user-generated content or chat

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, gold, unlocked items, settings and leaderboard numbers shown in the game (Weekly League and Headshot King) are stored only on your device. The other names in those in-game rankings are fictional opponents generated by the game; your score is never sent anywhere.

In-app purchases

The App offers optional purchases (Castle Key full version, Welcome Pack, gold packs and cosmetic items). Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Purchases are managed with RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information so your purchases can be verified and restored. RevenueCat never receives your payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Advertising

The App shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for example to continue after the castle falls, to double a level reward or to get extra ammo) and occasional interstitial ads between levels. Players who own the Castle Key do not see interstitial ads. To deliver ads, AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, coarse location derived from IP address and ad-interaction data, for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and — only if you allow tracking — ad personalization.

On first launch iOS asks for your permission with the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, you can still play everything and ads will not be personalized using activity from other companies' apps or websites. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are also asked for consent through Google's consent message. See policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Notifications

With your permission, the App schedules a small number of local reminder notifications directly on your device (for example, if you haven't played in a couple of days). These are generated and scheduled entirely on-device — no notification content or schedule is sent to us or to any third party. You can turn notifications off anytime in iOS Settings → Castle Bop → Notifications.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store — payments
  • RevenueCat — purchase management
  • Google AdMob — advertising

Your choices

  • Change tracking permission anytime: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Restore purchases anytime: in the game, Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Delete all game data by deleting the App, or with Settings → Reset Progress.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no social features or user-generated content.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
